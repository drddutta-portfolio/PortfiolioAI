# R4 original-statement format recovery — 5 November 2026

R4 remains partial. Last independently verified coverage is 2,029 / 2,096 eight-quarter slots with 49 historical R4 gaps and 18 separately deferred R3 gaps. A successful workflow does not establish financial completion.

This revision recognizes explicitly printed ordinal dates, scoped statutory Statement of Profit and Loss titles, and punctuation in the corresponding-previous-year quarter heading. An unreadable adjacent quarter can no longer invalidate an otherwise complete requested quarter when a different eligible quarter independently establishes the same numeric row; annual and cumulative columns cannot supply that proof. Requested financial cells remain strict.

Unreadable optional current/deferred tax components remain unknown. They can be bypassed only when an explicit readable total tax and PBT-minus-total-tax-equals-PAT independently reconcile the requested quarter. Core revenue, PAT and diluted EPS are never inferred or corrected. Original rows, issuer/scope checks, provenance and independent replay remain required.

Validation: 167 local Results tests pass. Actual SSWL and SANSERA originals still expose OCR limitations under investigation; neither is claimed recovered by this revision. Header-region and rotated-table OCR experiments are not enabled in this commit. No UI, database schema, RLS or Production changes.

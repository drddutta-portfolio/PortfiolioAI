## Methodology facts relevant to historical mapping

The official NSE methodology states that classification is based on the nature of revenue-generating business activities, uses audited consolidated annual financials as the prime source, classifies a multi-business company by a segment contributing more than 50% of total revenue, and defines Diversified treatment when no segment exceeds 50% under specified segment-contribution conditions.

Those official rules are preserved as source evidence. They are **not automatically adopted as PortfolioAI historical policy** because OD1–OD4 remain pending owner approval.

## Governing freshness audit

The BANK profile in `supabase/functions/_shared/p7-ic-profile-contracts.ts` (the BANK block, around lines 356–495) specifies 22 signals with `sourceAuthority=BANK_NBFC_STAGE_8_BANK_V1` but **does not supply a BANK freshnessPolicy**. Historical selected BANK items have `freshness_policy=null` for M1–M4. No universal 120-day/90-day permission arises from this fact.

The canonical `p7-ic-input-validation.ts` enforces exact `definition.period_type`, ISO end/start validity and source cutoffs. It limits `fresh_until <= retrieved_at + metric_definition.freshness_seconds` and requires `fresh_until >= evaluationAsOf`; it **does not intrinsically prevent an ancient reporting period from becoming apparently fresh on new retrieval**. `v14-reviewed-evidence.ts` requires explicit review source/fragment and supersession graph integrity. A new regulatory disclosure must not automatically erase a contradictory, unsuperseded review.

**One recommended BANK policy, proposed and NOT APPROVED:**

1. Financial-period applicability must be independent of retrieval age. For M1 (certified contiguous trailing 4 quarters) and M2/M3 (quarter-end regulatory as-of), set reporting-age applicability at **150 calendar days after period_end**, unless an explicitly qualified later same-perimeter disclosure supersedes sooner. This 150-day recommendation is grounded in the existing non-BANK policy precedent, not inherited approval. For M4 direct audited annual ROA, apply **550 calendar days after full audited fiscal year_end**, again subject to earlier supersession, consistent with the same external profile precedent.
2. Source-verification freshness is separate: require original immutable source hash, retrieved_at, source publication and review timestamp; proposed verification recheck cap **150 days** quarterly/regulatory, **550 days** annual, but a successful re-download can refresh transport verification only—never reset reporting-age applicability.
3. Suggested cap `fresh_until = min(period_end + applicable_reporting_age, source_verified_at + verification_cap)`, further truncated by explicit amended/next-period supersession effective time, new adverse/conflicting event, and review validity. `fresh_until` still uses the existing numeric validator cap and will require an aligned implementation/test, not merely registry value changes.
4. Publication date must not precede report_period_end; delayed availability is permitted with actual publication timestamp but may already leave short remaining applicability. Provider retrieval must not replace publication/report end.
5. If current owner-issued successor report is found (same metric/regulatory accounting perimeter) older one becomes historical—not current—once individually linked/admitted; amended exact-period issuer source supersedes only by explicit immutable link and conflict review. A document revision with differing figure and no authorized supersession means CONFLICTING, not newest-wins.
6. Retrieval failure is not a data deletion nor an automatic validity renewal. If verifiably current preexisting evidence remains within both clocks, keep historical factual review but report acquisition degraded; if either clock expires, fail closed.

**Boundary examples (proposed):** June 30 quarter downloaded October 10 has only up to Nov 27 (150 days from June 30), not March 9 next year. New September 30 approved report on October 25 supersedes June for current selection; June stays historical. September report first published December 20 is late but retains only its remaining reporting applicability to late February, not a fresh 150 days from December 20. September amended on November 15 with different CET1 cannot silently overwrite an earlier accepted fact; it enters conflict/reviewer-supersession. March 31 audited ROA remains eligible at most through the next year's October 2 under 550-day proposal unless the next audited FY is approved sooner; an unavailable annual report cannot be fabricated. A download failure on October 10 with June 30 report needs separate evidence of prior verification freshness.

**Activation decision required:** approve/reject exact dual-clock 150/550 BANK applicability, verification ceilings, calendar-day inclusivity, publication delay and issuer amendment/supersession rule. Until approval, keep all four `is_active=false`, do not use dormant 120/90 durations.

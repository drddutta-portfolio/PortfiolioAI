**Financial justification:** `PB_adj=PB*(ROE_reference/ROE_current)` can be unstable or misleading with equity leverage, cyclical credit provisions, a near-zero/currently negative denominator, merger-related book value changes and different accounting scopes. A residual-income justified P/B `(ROE-g)/(Ke-g)` presumes perpetual steady-state sustainable ROE and growth, `Ke>g`, consistent long-run payout/retention and capital, constant risk/equity cost, matching standalone/consolidated book value, no abnormal credit cycle or one-off earnings. The Gordon formulation derives from `P/B=(ROE-g)/(Ke-g)` under constant growth/clean surplus and a dividend payout supporting `g`. Bank-specific `Ke`, sustainable `g` and through-cycle ROE cannot be established by arbitrary fixed constants, so this alternative is not ready to score. Sensitivity illustration (synthetic only): ROE 15%, Ke 12%, g 5% => justified P/B 1.4286×; observed 2.0× implies 1.4× observed/justified. Ke 13% => justified 1.25×, observed/justified 1.6×; ROE 12% => justified 1×, observed/justified 2×; Ke<=g or ROE<=g invalid. These swings without validated regime assumptions justify eliminating M6 through a governed amendment rather than producing misleading precision.

**Decision-required consequences:** Under existing approved BANK V1, M6 **remains required and blocks all 13 READY**. If owner approves V2 retirement+reweighting **after weights and impacts are verified**, perform versioned profile migration (data/profiles per approved mechanism, not unapproved schema changes), audit score changes and source cutoffs, revalidate all 13 at same frozen monetary baseline. This is the single recommended choice; there is no proposal to activate the earlier ROE-normalization heuristic.

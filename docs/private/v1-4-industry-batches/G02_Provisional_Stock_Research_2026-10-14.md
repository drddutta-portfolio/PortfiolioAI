## CGPOWER

**Issuer-reported evidence.** The FY2025–26 chairman's message reports consolidated revenue of ₹12,418 crore, growth of 25%, and PAT of ₹1,196 crore. The managing director's message reports consolidated order intake of ₹19,616 crore and unexecuted order book of ₹17,107 crore. These narrative figures must be reconciled with audited statements and exceptional-item treatment before admission; do not combine standalone order backlog with the consolidated figures.

The standalone key-ratio table separately reports operating profit margin excluding other income of 13.64% for FY26 and 12.71% for FY25. Issuer terminal ROCE excluding exceptional items is 22.08% versus 33.34%; management explains that investment of QIP proceeds increased capital employed. Interest coverage is 85.77 versus 90.59. These are specific standalone definitions, not a consolidated margin series, average-capital ROCE or complete three-year leverage contract. The reported debt/equity figure is not proof that every consolidated debt obligation is zero.

**Interpretation.** The available evidence supports a constructive preliminary view of power-equipment demand and order visibility. The capital-efficiency decline needs to be understood alongside newly raised capital and investment deployment, rather than being interpreted automatically as deterioration in the underlying franchise. Semiconductor expansion creates a distinct capital-allocation and execution exposure; proposed capacity is not realised production or profit.

**Governance evidence:** the report discloses continuing CBI, SFIO and ED investigations concerning prior management. The qualified governance review retains this unresolved risk; it does not establish clean governance or absence of litigation.

**What could change the opinion.** Order conversion, cash collection and capacity execution must support the reported growth. A strong backlog cannot compensate for weak cash conversion. Valuation has not been qualified, so the business assessment does not establish that the shares are attractively priced.

**Provisional opinion:** merits continued research on business and order visibility; investment attractiveness remains unestablished pending cash-flow, comparable return-on-capital and valuation evidence. Confidence is limited to the source-backed findings above.

## AVALON

**Issuer-reported evidence.** The FY26 management performance message reports revenue of ₹1,603 crore, EBITDA of ₹173 crore, EBITDA margin of 10.8%, PAT of ₹113 crore and ROCE of 20.6% versus 15.7%. It reports net working capital of 112 days versus 124 and operating cash flow of ₹57 crore. These narrative observations require reconciliation to the applicable audited scope and ROCE definition before numeric admission. EBITDA margin is not substituted for the frozen operating-margin contract.

Management describes box-build share rising from 44% four years earlier to 54%, and an order book of ₹2,196 crore entering FY27. Qualification discipline, engineering involvement and customer-program ramp-up are documented. These figures are not a verified four-period order series or eight-quarter growth history. An alternative annual-report PDF format is not an additional reporting year.

**Interpretation.** Increasing system complexity and the India/US manufacturing platform could deepen customer relationships. Working-capital improvement is encouraging as an issuer claim, but cash conversion needs a consistent multi-year assessment. Program readiness does not guarantee timely volume production, and an order book does not guarantee revenue or cash collection.

**What could change the opinion.** Customer dependence, ramp-up delays, geographical demand changes and sustained cash generation need source-backed investigation. The recorded heavy-electrical classification should be assessed for suitability through the approved process; its present assignment does not establish economic comparability with CGPOWER or TDPOWERSYS.

**Provisional opinion:** promising complexity and execution narrative with material ramp-up and cash-conversion questions. No valuation conclusion, target price or investment recommendation is established.

## TDPOWERSYS

**Issuer-reported evidence.** The FY26 report describes generators for steam, gas, hydro, diesel, geothermal and wind applications and OEM/utility relationships. Management's consolidated financial discussion reports sales of ₹1,85,623.38 lakh for FY26 versus ₹1,27,876.17 lakh for FY25. Its standalone sales discussion uses different amounts; those series must remain separate.

Management reports FY26 order inflows of ₹2,23,848.20 lakh and says the top ten customers contributed 84% of consolidated revenue in both FY26 and FY25. Exports including deemed exports are reported as 79% of order inflow. This provides explicit concentration exposure even though end applications are varied.

**Source anomaly requiring resolution.** The pending-order-book paragraph labels both the current ₹1,97,288.60 lakh figure and the ₹1,36,801.40 lakh comparative with “April 01, 2026”. The separate opening-FY26 paragraph helps identify context, but this report does not silently correct the contradictory date or admit it as a dated two-period observation. Obtain a corroborating dated issuer disclosure.

The standalone ROCE note defines its numerator as PAT plus depreciation/amortisation plus finance cost and denominator as shareholder equity plus deferred-tax liabilities plus total debt. Do not assume this equals the standard EBIT/average-capital formula or compare it directly with CGPOWER's terminal issuer ROCE. This definitional issue is qualification work, not absence of a source value.

**Interpretation.** The documented OEM relationships and generator applications support business research. Customer concentration, export/deemed-export exposure, delivery schedules and cash collection deserve particular attention. Different issuer ROCE definitions prevent an honest peer ranking from the retained ratios alone.

**Provisional opinion:** credible equipment-business evidence with concentrated customer and execution exposure; cash conversion and valuation are unresolved. The current data supports monitoring and further qualification, not a completed buy/hold/sell opinion.

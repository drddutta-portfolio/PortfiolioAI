## Debt and interest coverage — approved component admission

Require three matched annual periods of both net-debt/EBITDA and EBIT/finance-cost coverage. Net debt includes verified interest-bearing borrowings and lease liabilities, less verified unrestricted cash/cash equivalents; restricted balances, customer advances and trade payables must not silently change the definition. EBITDA and EBIT must use compatible approved definitions with the same accounting scope. Include lease interest in total finance costs where leases are in debt. A printed dash, 'debt-free' narrative or debt-service-coverage ratio is not a numeric zero or interest coverage. Non-positive EBITDA or zero/unproven finance costs leaves the corresponding ratio blocked pending an explicit applicability rule; there is no invented infinity or blanket exemption.
